Recycling Quiz 🎯

Test your knowledge and earn eco-points for every correct answer!

1. Where should you put an empty plastic water bottle?
✅ Correct! +50 eco-points! Plastic bottles go in the recyclable bin — just rinse them first!
❌ Incorrect. Plastic bottles should go in the ♻️ Recyclable bin after rinsing.
2. You have a pizza box with grease stains. What do you do?
✅ Correct! +50 points! Greasy cardboard cannot be recycled. Tear off and recycle the clean top, trash the greasy bottom.
❌ Greasy cardboard contaminates recycling batches. It goes in general waste.
3. Old phone batteries should go...
✅ Correct! +50 points! Batteries contain toxic chemicals. Always take them to a designated hazardous drop-off.
❌ Batteries are hazardous! They belong at a ☢️ hazardous waste collection point.
4. Vegetable peels from cooking should go in...
✅ Correct! +50 points! Food waste like vegetable peels are perfect for the organic bin and composting.
❌ Vegetable peels are organic waste. They go in the 🌿 Organic / compost bin.
